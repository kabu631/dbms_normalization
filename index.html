Quick Revision — Normalization Reference

6-Step Normalization Checklist

Step 1
Atomicity (1NF)
Ensure each column domain contains indivisible scalar values. Eliminate repeating groups and nested tables by unnesting rows and establishing composite keys.
Step 2
Candidate Keys
Compute attribute closures (X+) starting with non-RHS essential attributes. Find minimal superkeys whose closure spans all attributes in R.
Step 3
Eliminate Partials (2NF)
No non-prime attribute may functionally depend on a strict proper subset of any candidate key (X ⊂ CK). Isolate partial FDs into separate relations.
Step 4
Eliminate Transitives (3NF)
For every non-trivial X → Y, either X must be a Superkey OR Y must be Prime. Remove transitive dependencies (non-key → non-key) into master tables.
Step 5
Foreign Key Integrity
Explicitly define Primary Keys (PK) and Foreign Keys (FK) linking child transaction tables back to parent entity tables.
Step 6
Lossless & Preservation
Verify that R1 ∩ R2 forms a superkey of R1 or R2 (Lossless Join) and that the union of local FDs preserves the full closure F+.
